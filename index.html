About

I’m an entrepreneur interested in what happens when powerful technologies meet problems that matter. I care about making AI useful to more people, while also taking its risks and social consequences seriously.

My background spans humanitarian medicine, finance, operations, and company building. That mix has made me especially drawn to neglected problems: the ones that fall between disciplines, markets, and institutions. I like turning unconventional ideas into practical projects and working with people who care as much about consequences as possibilities.
