Manage Work & Research

Use this page whenever you want to publish a document on your website. No code editing is required.

  1. Click Add new work and sign in to GitHub if asked.
  2. Write the title of your work.
  3. Complete the category and short description.
  4. In the Document box, drag in a PDF, paste a public link, or paste the text of the work.
  5. Click Submit new issue. The item will appear automatically on your website.

Everything you submit is public. Edit an open issue to update an item. Close the issue when you want to remove it from the website.
